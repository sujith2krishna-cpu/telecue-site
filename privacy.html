1. About TeleCue

TeleCue is an Android app that records video while showing a scrolling script (teleprompter) on screen. The script is not part of the saved video.

2. What the app uses and why

  • Camera and microphone: to show the camera preview and to record the videos you choose to record. Nothing is recorded until you press the record button.
  • Storage permission (Android 10 and older only): to save your videos to your gallery.
  • Internet access: used only by Google Play Billing to show plans and process purchases.

3. Data stored on your device

Your videos are saved to your phone's gallery (album "TeleCue"). While a video is being recorded it is written to the app's private cache folder, and a copy may stay there after it is saved to your gallery until Android clears the cache or you clear it yourself.

Your script text, the date your free trial started (plus the last time the app was opened, used to check the trial) and whether you have bought a plan are stored on your device only. We do not receive any of this.

4. Data we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. TeleCue has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the advertising ID. The app does not upload your videos or script anywhere.

Purchases use Google's Play Billing library, which communicates with Google Play and may send Google technical information needed to process and diagnose purchases. This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy (see section 5); we do not receive it.

5. Purchases

Payments for the lifetime, monthly and yearly plans are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google's own privacy policy applies to purchases: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Children

TeleCue is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

7. Deleting your data

Delete your videos from your gallery app. Uninstalling TeleCue removes the script, trial and plan information stored by the app, along with any temporary recordings in its cache. You can also remove temporary recordings at any time in Settings > Apps > TeleCue > Storage > Clear cache.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
